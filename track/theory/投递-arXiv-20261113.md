## 一、要填的字段（照抄）

- **Title**：Why AGI Agents Cannot Work: The First Cause of Life and a General Problem Theory
- **Authors**：Zijie Rao
- **Abstract**（arXiv 上限约 1920 字符，下面这段 1 段式，可直接粘）：
  Almost everyone discussing AGI asks how capable a system can become. This paper asks a different question: what makes something count as alive. Its claim is one sentence: intelligence is a consequence of life, and a heartbeat is where evolution starts. Four claims are made, each paired with something runnable: (1) the first cause of life is survival — "must continue" is not an imposed rule but an entailment; (2) being alive equals one complete loop run end to end, that is, one heartbeat; (3) four breakpoints (machine swap, plugin removal, rebuild, model swap) serve as identity tests, two of which have been measured on a running system; (4) a general problem theory: the problem is universal while solutions are substrate-dependent. Unlike work that evaluates identity facts, governed state transitions, or runtime authorization, this paper treats continuation itself as the object to be verified, and reports a running implementation together with an explicit list of what is not finished.
