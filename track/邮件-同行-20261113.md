## 一、英文模板（150 词，直接可用）

**Subject**: A runnable test for the gap your review names ("couplings among mechanisms")

Dear Dr. ___,

Your review *From Cognitive Architectures to Language Agents* ends with a line I have not been able to forget: "The strongest remaining opportunities lie in couplings among mechanisms." I built that coupling as a single chain and made it runnable.

Concretely: a three-second heartbeat; one complete loop per beat (read → judge → dispatch → write back); an immune layer with adversarial probes; and four breakpoints (machine swap, plugin removal, rebuild, model swap) as identity tests — two measured, two not. The paper also states what is missing, including that the consciousness layer is unfinished.

Preprint (English): [arXiv link once posted] · Chinese version with appendices: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24151620

Two asks, both small:
1. Would you be willing to endorse me for cs.AI on arXiv? (If not, no problem at all.)
2. If you think the chain is wrong somewhere, I would rather hear it now than after publication.

Thank you for the review — it is the reason I could name what I was doing.

Best regards,
Zijie Rao (independent researcher)
